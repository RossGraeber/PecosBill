3. Montering — assembly

1

CNC the deck (3) and the steel-lid drill template from one 18 mm ply sheet. Two motor cutouts, inlet, relief, box mounts.

2

Clamp template to the steel lid (2), drill and cut. Deburr both faces — hole edges are the lid's only stress raisers (P4.95).

3

Through-bolt deck (3) onto lid (2), fender washers below, foam tape between. Steel seals, ply stiffens (P11.32).

4

TPU ring (4) into each seat; motor (5) fan-eye-down onto ring; clamp ring (6) over tabs, 3 × M5. Vacuum self-seats it when running (P11.33).

5

Under the lid: snorkel + gravity flap (7) onto each motor eye, cloth sleeve (8) over snorkel. Idle motor = sealed motor (P11.14, P11.34).

6

Exhaust elbows off both horns into the common manifold (9), foam-lined, one 2.5″ outlet aimed down. Heat, brush dust, noise — away (P11.35).

7

Box (12): cord (13) → fuses → contactor (24 V coil ← CNC out, verify VDC vs VAC). Motor 2 through the on-delay — two inrushes never share a half-second (P11.37–P11.38).

8

Lid assembly onto drum (1), bolt ring (14) closed, relief valve (11) in, hose (15) from cyclone, plug into the 20 A outlet. First run: one motor + clamp meter (Q-V5).
