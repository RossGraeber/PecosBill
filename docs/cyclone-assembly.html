3. Montering — assembly

1

Print all 20 parts: cone courses/arcs wide-end down, roof arcs roof-down, duct and adapter flange/sleeve-down. PETG, 0.45 mm loops × 5, no supports. ~5.4 kg total.

2

Ring up each split course on the bench: foam tape on the web faces, M4 through the mating webs — 3 bolts per seam. Snug, don't crush: seams are in compression in service (P8.100). Four rings: (3) (4) (5) (6).

3

Stack bottom-up, (1) → (6): foam on each flange face, drop the upper course into the lower course's register lip, bolt the flange ring. Bolt counts grow with radius (6…12) — every hole gets a bolt (P7.14).

4

Bolt the inlet duct (9) to the frame on course_06_arc2: foam gasket, 6 × M4, U-pattern (roof caps the top edge). The morph is a diffuser — hose 21 m/s in, 12.5 m/s at the scroll (P12.07).

5

Drum lid (12): Ø 90 mm centre hole, 6 × 5.5 mm on the 122 mm bolt circle. Deburr both faces — the hole edge is the lid's one stress raiser (P4.95). Bolt the lid socket (10) around it, gasket between.

6

Cone tip (1) slides into the socket (10), 0.3 mm/side. Set the assembled body on, close the drum bolt ring. Check the drum gasket — the bin is the dominant loss path (rank 2, P4.10).

7

Drop the vortex finder (7) through the roof bore, collar seated. Slip the outlet adapter (8) over the 45 mm stub — friction fit + foam wrap. Both re-print alone if `De` gets re-tuned (F2).

8

Hoses (15): CNC boot → inlet spigot, adapter → SC0075 — short, sloped or vertical (P12.08). First run: chips in the drum, near-nothing in the bag. Measure sealed lift + free flow while you're there (Q-H2).
